# Provenance — Nagao j=1728 Rank ≥4

Primary authority: Koh-ichi Nagao, *On the rank of elliptic curve
y²=x³-kx*, Kobe J. Math. 11 (1994), 205–210.

Theorem 1 proves infinitely many pairwise non-Q-isomorphic curves of this form
with rank at least 4. Section 2 prints the four rational functions
a(T),b(T),c(T),d(T), their equal-biquadrate identity, k(T), and the four points.

The PDF text extraction renders the denominator in the factored k(T) formula as
"/224". It cannot literally mean 224. Direct exact reconstruction from
k=a^4+b^4 shows the denominator is 2^24, and this is independently confirmed at
T=3 by the paper's integer control
(a,b,c,d)=(133,-134,158,-59) and
k=41·113·241·569.

Nagao proves independence at T=3, then independence over Q(T), and finally
independence for almost all rational specializations via Silverman's theorem.

The paper proves a lower bound, not exact generic rank 4. Rank Hunter locally
re-certifies the four directions at T=3 and preserves the theorem as provenance.

For current j=1728 record context, a 2026 Dujella–Najman prescribed-rank survey
cites Watkins's curve
y²=x³-402599774387690701016910427272483 x
as rank 14 over Q.
