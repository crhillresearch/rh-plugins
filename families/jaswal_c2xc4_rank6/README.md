# Jaswal C2 × C4 genus-one family

Source: [Jaswal, *Infinitely many elliptic curves with torsion Z/2Z × Z/4Z and rank at least six* (2026)](https://hexagonmath.org/pdf/2610.00586v1). This plugin records the first genus-one base and its exact curve model. The paper also gives a second genus-one family of rank six; it needs a separate exact model and is not represented by this `family.json`.

The six-section theorem is cited as historical evidence. The repo's independent reconstruction and fiber calculations are in `research/z4/certify_jaswal_c4_isogeny_families.py` and `research/z4/jaswal_c4_isogeny_family_certificates.json`.
