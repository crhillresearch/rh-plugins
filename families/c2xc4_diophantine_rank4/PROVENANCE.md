# Provenance — Dujella–Peral C2 × C4 rank-4 family

Primary source: Dujella–Peral (2014), LMS J. Comput. Math. 17, 282–288.

The plugin transcribes the paper's final cleared A(t), factored B(t), and the
four x-coordinates X1,...,X4. The paper uses t=2 for independence and t=15 for
the Gusić–Tadić injective/full-basis argument. At t=15 the specialized rank is
4 and the four specialized points form a full basis, proving exact generic rank
4 and that the displayed points are free generators.

The rank-9 rational curve later in the paper arises from the wider
four-parameter search and is not a second one-parameter rank-4 family variant.

The maintained Dujella prescribed-torsion table, checked 2026-10-02, lists best
known lower bound 9 for C2 × C4. Rank Hunter targets 10.
