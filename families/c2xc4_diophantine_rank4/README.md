# Dujella–Peral · Z/2 × Z/4 · Diophantine Triple Rank 4

This Family reconstructs the 2014 Dujella–Peral one-parameter family induced by
a rational Diophantine triple with torsion `Z/2Z × Z/4Z` and exact generic rank 4.

The paper prints the final cleared model and four points. Specialization at
`t=2` shows those directions are independent; the Gusić–Tadić criterion at
`t=15` proves generic rank exactly 4 and that the four points form a full free
basis.

Rank Hunter exact-certifies those four directions at `t=15`. Candidate
Generation, Family Search and Target Search are all supported. Search is
write-free and returns typed point artifacts; core owns validation, exact
independence and rank promotion.

The maintained prescribed-torsion table still lists rank at least 9 for
`Z/2Z × Z/4Z`, so record-oriented searches target rank 10.

Source: A. Dujella and J. C. Peral, *High rank elliptic curves with torsion
Z/2Z × Z/4Z induced by Diophantine triples*, LMS J. Comput. Math. 17 (2014),
282–288.
