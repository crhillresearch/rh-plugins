# Mathematical provenance and claim boundary

Stephanie Chan, Jeroen Hanselman, and Wanlin Li, “Ranks, 2-Selmer groups, and
Tamagawa numbers of elliptic curves with Z/2Z × Z/8Z-torsion,” *Open Book
Series* 2 (2019), 173–189, [arXiv:1805.10709](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.10709),
present the C2 × C8 parametrization and attribute it to Bartosz Naskręcki.

The signed squareclass restriction used here follows directly from the
classical factorization `Y²=X(X+AZ²)(X+BZ²)` and primitive denominators. It is
a necessary condition on `X`, not a new rank theorem. The channel organization
is inspired by the user's REA research workflow; this family is not an
REA-derived cubic.

The plugin claims only exact candidate points and bounded enumeration coverage.
Rank Hunter core independently verifies the points and may certify free rank
growth. Neither a new squareclass nor a new denominator is an independence
certificate. No result here supplies a rank upper bound.
