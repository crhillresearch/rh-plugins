# Provenance — Z/8 rank-2 families

Primary authority: Dujella–Kazalicki–Peral (2021), §§4.1–4.8 and Theorem 1.
The theorem states that the seven displayed Z/8 families have exact rank 2 over
Q(u), and that the two listed points together with torsion generate the full
Mordell–Weil group. The injective controls are 22, 19, 11, 17, 3, -48, 10.

The plugin transcribes the final cleared polynomial models and final free-basis
x-coordinate formulas. In §4.7 the final squared quintic is the denominator of
the second x-coordinate; exact curve membership at u=10 confirms this reading.

Paper exact-rank metadata never substitutes for specialized Rank Hunter proof:
candidate torsion and rank are recomputed exactly.
