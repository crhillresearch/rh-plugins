# Z/8 Rank-2 Families · Dujella–Kazalicki–Peral

Seven pairwise non-isomorphic one-parameter `Z/8Z` families whose exact
generic rank 2 and full free generators are established in Theorem 1 of
Dujella–Kazalicki–Peral (2021).

Variants: Dujella–Peral I (`u=22`), Dujella–Peral II (`u=19`), New I
(`u=11`), MacLeod I (`u=17`), MacLeod II (`u=3`), New II (`u=-48`),
and New III (`u=10`).

Every variant supports Candidate Generation, Family Search and Target Search.
The shared write-free runner reconstructs the selected variant's two published
free generators, searches staged completed-square ratpoints for extras, and
returns typed point artifacts to Rank Hunter core for validation and exact rank
promotion.

The theorem's exact generic-rank-2 statement remains provenance. Rank Hunter
locally exact-certifies the two free directions at each paper control and
requires exact specialized `C8` torsion/rank evidence for candidates.

Source: Andrej Dujella, Matija Kazalicki, Juan Carlos Peral,
*Elliptic curves with torsion groups Z/8Z and Z/2Z × Z/6Z*,
RACSAM 115 (2021), Article 169; arXiv:2105.06215.
